The rule that never inverts

It only ever adds permission. Go over for the week and you get one factual line and nothing else — no eating less, no making up for it. The moment "earned" has an opposite it's a punishment schedule, and that's what turns a food log into a disorder. If the log ever starts looking like someone eating too little, the whole frame switches off and the coaching stops.
