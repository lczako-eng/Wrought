Privacy

Last updated 8 August 2026

Wrought holds what you eat, what you lift, what you weigh, and whatever your watch reports. That is about as personal as data gets, so this page is written to be read rather than to be legally survivable. If anything here is unclear, ask and it gets rewritten.

The short version

Your data is never sold, never rented, never used to train anyone's model, and never shared for advertising. It is stored in one database that only you can read. You can export every row or delete the whole account at any time, without asking us.

There is no analytics script, no advertising pixel, no third-party tracker and no session recording on any page of this site.

Who else sees it

Four companies process data on Wrought's behalf. There are no others, and none of them are advertising companies.

Worth being specific about

When you say "chicken burrito bowl, no rice", that sentence is sent to OpenAI so it can be turned into calories, macros and categories. Your nightly verdict is written the same way, from numbers already calculated here.

OpenAI's API terms state that data submitted through the API is not used to train their models. Even so — this is a real third party seeing real sentences about your life, and you should know that before you type anything you would not want processed by a company that is not us.

What is never done

  • Never sold or rented. Not to insurers, employers, advertisers, data brokers or researchers. There is no version of this product where that changes.
  • Never used to train a model, ours or anyone else's.
  • Never shared with an assistant you have not connected. Connecting ChatGPT does not give Claude access, and neither of them can see another user's anything.
  • Never used to profile you for advertising. There is no advertising.

Your control

Take everything

GET /api/export returns every row Wrought holds about you as JSON, or ?format=csv for spreadsheets. There is a button on the connect page. No request, no waiting period, no export fee. A hub you cannot leave is a trap.

Delete everything

Deleting your account removes every row — log, sets, metrics, routines, briefs, memory, connections — by database cascade, not by a flag that hides it. Email laszlobrianczako@gmail.com and it is done within 7 days. Backups roll off within 30.

Revoke access

Disconnect an assistant and its token stops working immediately. Revoke a device key on the connect page and the phone using it stops being able to write. Neither affects your data.

Where it lives, and how long

Data is stored in Supabase's cloud infrastructure. Wrought is operated from Canada, so PIPEDA applies; if you are in the EU or UK, GDPR and UK GDPR give you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability — all of which are satisfied by the export and delete above, and none of which require a form.

Your log is kept until you delete it, because that is the entire product — a record that forgets after ninety days is not a record. Server logs are kept 30 days. Nothing is archived after account deletion.

Health data, specifically

Health information is sensitive-category data under GDPR Article 9 and is treated as such. The legal basis is your explicit consent, given by connecting an assistant or a device, and withdrawable at any time by disconnecting or deleting.

Wrought is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or advise on any condition, and it is not a substitute for a doctor or dietitian. It will not interpret your heart rate, HRV or bloodwork as a clinical sign, and it is built to say so and point you at a professional when a question crosses that line.

Security

  • Every table has row-level security; your rows are readable only by your account.
  • Access tokens, refresh tokens and device keys are stored as SHA-256 hashes. A database dump yields no usable credential.
  • Device keys are write-only — a key on your phone can add health data and cannot read anything back or touch your login.
  • All traffic is HTTPS. OAuth uses PKCE, and refresh tokens rotate on every use.

No system is perfect. If something is breached that affects you, you will be told directly and promptly — not buried in a status page.

Children

Wrought is not for anyone under 16 and is not directed at children. If an account is found to belong to someone younger, it is deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is handled, you will be emailed before it takes effect — not after, and not only on this page.
