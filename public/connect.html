Connect it

Two things to set up. The AI takes a minute; the watch takes two.

1 · Put FORGE inside your AI

FORGE is an MCP server, which means any assistant that speaks MCP can use it — you add one URL and sign in once. After that it is simply there, in every conversation, forever.

The URL

https://forge.fit/mcp

ChatGPT

  1. Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Paste the URL above. Leave authentication as OAuth.
  3. Sign in when the FORGE page appears. That is the only time you will see it.

Claude

  1. Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Paste the URL. Claude registers itself and opens the sign-in.

Anything else

Any MCP client works — the server advertises OAuth 2.1 with dynamic client registration at /.well-known/oauth-authorization-server, so a conforming client needs nothing but the URL.

Then just talk to it:

"eggs and black coffee, 182 on the scale"
"pushed 40 minutes upper body, bench 3x8 at 80"
"how's the month looking?"
"what should I eat, I've got 20 minutes"
"my left knee goes if I squat below parallel"   ← said once, remembered forever

2 · Let your watch do the boring half

Sleep, steps, resting heart rate and weight should not be things you type. Connect a device and they arrive on their own overnight.

Why Apple works differently. Apple Health has no cloud API — there is no entitlement, no partner programme and no price at which any server can read an Apple Watch. The data lives on your phone and leaves only if your phone sends it. So instead of us reading your watch, your phone pushes to us once a night. Every "Apple Health integration" you have ever used is this underneath; most of them just do not say so.

Get your device key

Sign in to mint a key for this phone.

Option A — the Shortcut (free, five taps)

  1. Open Shortcuts on the iPhone → Automation → new Personal Automation.
  2. Trigger: Time of Day → 11:00 pm → Run Immediately.
  3. Add Find Health Samples. Set it to Steps, today. Repeat the action for Sleep Analysis, Resting Heart Rate, Heart Rate Variability, Active Energy and Body Mass.
  4. Add Get Contents of URL, method POST, pointed at https://forge.fit/ingest.
  5. Headers: Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY and Content-Type: application/json.
  6. Request body → JSON, in this shape:
{
  "source": "apple_health",
  "metrics": [
    { "metric": "steps",         "value": 8412, "unit": "count", "measured_at": "2026-08-08T23:00:00Z" },
    { "metric": "sleep_minutes", "value": 402,  "unit": "min",   "measured_at": "2026-08-08T07:00:00Z" },
    { "metric": "resting_hr",    "value": 54,   "unit": "bpm",   "measured_at": "2026-08-08T07:00:00Z" },
    { "metric": "weight_kg",     "value": 82.6, "unit": "kg",    "measured_at": "2026-08-08T07:15:00Z" }
  ]
}

Run it once to check, then forget about it. Re-sending the same night twice is harmless — duplicates are dropped on arrival, so a Shortcut that fires twice never doubles your sleep.

Option B — Health Auto Export (no building)

The Health Auto Export app on the App Store posts the same data on a schedule with nothing to assemble. Point its REST endpoint at /ingest, add the same Authorization: Bearer header, and FORGE understands its format natively.

Rings, straps and scales

Oura, Whoop, Fitbit, Garmin and Withings all have real cloud APIs and will connect over OAuth — those are a build item here, not something waiting on you. In the meantime, most of them write into Apple Health on the iPhone, which means the Shortcut above already picks their data up today.

Your data

Everything lives in one Supabase database with row-level security, so your rows are reachable only by you. A device key can write health data and nothing else — it cannot read anything back and cannot touch your login. Revoke a key here and the phone using it stops working immediately. Delete your account and the rows go with it.
