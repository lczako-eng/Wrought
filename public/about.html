Wrought

About

The word, the product, and the sentences that run it

The name

Wrought is the old past tense of work — it is what you call iron after it has been through the fire and the hammer enough times to hold a shape. Nothing about wrought iron happened in one session. That is the whole idea: a body, like the metal, is worked — repeatedly, honestly, over a long time — and the product's job is to hold the record of that work so none of it is lost.

What it is

WROUGHT is the memory your AI doesn't have. You already talk to ChatGPT or Claude. You mention what you ate, what you lifted, what the scale said — and tomorrow it has forgotten all of it. Connect WROUGHT once and everything you mention is kept: every meal, every set, every weigh-in, filed under the right day, forever. Then it reads the record back to you honestly — totals, trends, and a nightly verdict on how the week is actually going.

It is not another app to open. The AI you already use is the interface; WROUGHT is the memory behind it, plus a dashboard when you want to look at your own record laid out.

Your gym, from a photograph

Take a few pictures of the gym you train in and show them to your AI. It can see what is standing there — the racks, the machines, the dumbbells — and tells WROUGHT the equipment list. From then on every session it builds uses what that gym actually has. Train in more than one place? Photograph both, name them, and say where you are: "I'm at the home gym today."

WROUGHT never sees the photograph. Your AI reads it; only the equipment list arrives here. The same is true of pictures of your food.

What it will not do

  • It will not flatter you. The nightly read is honest — that is the point of it. Hard on the behaviour, never on the person. You choose the register: gentle, honest, or brutal.
  • It will not guess a working weight. Loads come from your own history. No history means it prescribes effort, not a number a stranger invented.
  • It will not moralise a fast, score your streak of self-denial, or punish a missed week. Sessions never roll over. Guilt is how training logs die.
  • It stops coaching when the numbers look unsafe — eating too little, losing too fast, no rest in a fortnight. Care outranks coaching, always.
  • It is not a medical device. No diagnosis, no medication advice. It will say so and point at a doctor.
Your record is yours

Every meal, set, metric and verdict comes back on demand as JSON or CSV from the dashboard — always, even if you stop paying for anything, ever. A memory you cannot take with you is a trap, and this is not one.

Get started
